## The name

**Nolad** stands for **Nothing Lost After Deadline**. A Kaggle competition ends on a fixed date; the solutions produced in its last weeks are often the strongest methods available for the problem, and without documentation most of that work is lost once attention moves to the next competition. The name is also older than its expansion: it was coined first and given its meaning when this project needed one.

## Why Kaggle solutions

Kaggle code competitions impose limits that resemble real deployment. A typical medical imaging competition allows nine to twelve hours on a pair of mid-range GPUs to process a hidden test set, with no internet. BirdCLEF 2024 allowed two hours on a CPU. The American Sign Language fingerspelling competition required a TensorFlow Lite file under 40 MB. A solution that wins under such limits already carries evidence that it can run where compute is scarce, which is the situation in most of the world's hospitals and laboratories.

The limits also force a kind of honesty. Every solution is scored on data its authors never saw, under the same rules as everyone else. Published papers rarely offer that comparison.

The competitions have weaknesses too, and the reports name them: small test sets that reward luck, metrics that reward tricks a practitioner would not want, and ensembles too large to deploy. The deployability card on each report is there to separate the method from the competition artefacts.

## Who it is for

Students and engineers who want to learn how strong solutions are built; practitioners in each field, such as clinicians, teachers and scientists, who want to know what these methods can and cannot do; and competitors who want the accumulated knowledge of past competitions in one place. Each report is written so that a reader with no machine learning background can follow it by opening the explanatory boxes, and an expert can skip them.

## Hausdorff Space

Nolad is a project of [Hausdorff Space](https://hausdorff-space.vercel.app), a Nairobi intellectual collective that takes apart research papers, patents and mathematical ideas and makes them accessible. Nolad applies the same method to machine learning solutions.

## Credit and licences

The methods described in every report belong to their authors, who are named with links to their write-ups and code. Nolad does not copy repositories or datasets. Short code excerpts are quoted for explanation under the licences their authors chose, with a link to the source.
