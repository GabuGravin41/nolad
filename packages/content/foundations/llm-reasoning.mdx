### Next-token prediction

A decoder-only transformer assigns a probability to each possible next token given all previous tokens, and is trained to maximise the log-likelihood of text:

$$
\log P(t_1,\dots,t_T) = \sum_{k=1}^{T}\log P(t_k\mid t_1,\dots,t_{k-1}).
$$

Generation samples tokens one at a time. **Temperature** $T$ divides the logits before the softmax: $T\to0$ approaches greedy decoding; larger $T$ gives more varied samples.

### Chain of thought

A model fine-tuned on problems paired with long step-by-step solutions (chain of thought, CoT) learns to produce reasoning before the answer. Longer reasoning costs more tokens and therefore more time. In the AIMO report, the NemoSkills team generated 3.2 million solutions with DeepSeek-R1 and QwQ-32B, kept those whose final answers matched, and fine-tuned a 14-billion-parameter model on them.

### Tool-integrated reasoning

Arithmetic and case enumeration are error-prone in text. In **tool-integrated reasoning (TIR)**, the model writes a code block, generation pauses, a sandbox executes the code, and its output is appended to the context before generation resumes. The model can check a conjecture by brute force or compute a sum exactly.

### Majority voting

Sampling $k$ independent solutions and returning the most common final answer (maj@$k$) is more accurate than a single sample when the model is right more often than any particular wrong answer. If the probability of the correct answer exceeds that of every competitor, the majority converges to it as $k$ grows. pass@$k$, the probability that at least one of $k$ samples is right, measures the ceiling that a perfect selector could reach.

### Fine-tuning at scale

Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) continues training a pretrained model on target-format data with the same next-token loss. Two further techniques appear in the AIMO report: extending the positional encoding (raising the RoPE base) so the model can handle long reasoning traces, and **model merging**, a linear interpolation of the weights of two fine-tuned versions of the same base model.
