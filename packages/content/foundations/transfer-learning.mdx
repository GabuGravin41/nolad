### Supervised pretraining

A backbone trained to classify ImageNet photographs has learned filters for edges, textures and shapes. Fine-tuning it on medical images starts from those filters instead of random numbers, converges faster and overfits less. This is standard in almost every image solution here.

When the new task looks nothing like the pretraining data, what transfers is mostly low-level. A pretraining task closer to the target helps more:

- The aneurysm winner pretrained his classifier's backbone on **vessel segmentation** in the same scans; removing that pretraining cut his score from 0.902 to 0.794, the largest effect in his ablation.
- The lumbar spine winner pretrained coordinate models on a community dataset of spine landmarks, which beat ImageNet initialisation.

### Self-supervised pretraining

Labels are expensive; raw data are not. Self-supervised methods invent a task from the data itself:

- **Masked modelling:** hide parts of the input and predict them. BERT hides 15% of tokens and predicts them from context; the BELKA winner did the same with SMILES strings of molecules.
- **Self-distillation (DINO, iBOT):** train a network so that its output on one augmented view matches a slowly updated copy's output on another view. Owkin's Phikon, used by the UBC-OCEAN winners, is a ViT-Base trained with iBOT on 40 million tiles from The Cancer Genome Atlas.
- **Predicting a derived quantity:** the BELKA winner's second pretraining stage predicted each molecule's ECFP fingerprint from its SMILES string, a task hard enough to force the encoder to learn chemical structure.

### Foundation models as feature extractors

A **foundation model** is a large pretrained network used for many downstream tasks, often frozen. In UBC-OCEAN, every tile of a whole-slide image was passed through Phikon to produce a 768-dimensional embedding, and only a small multiple-instance model was trained on top. In CAFA 5, protein language models (ESM, ProtT5) turned each amino-acid sequence into an embedding for downstream classifiers. Training costs fall from days of GPU time to minutes, and small labelled datasets become usable.

### Licences travel with weights

Pretrained weights come with licences. SegFormer's weights, Phikon's weights and some ESM models restrict commercial use. A model fine-tuned from them inherits the restriction, which is recorded on the relevant deployability cards.
